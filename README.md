# Remix of Remix of Lumina Islamic

Hello, I'd like to create an Islamic app called "Islami." I want it to be available in 100 languages and 100 countries. However, I will start developing it with Arabic as the primary language. I want the app to be modern, elegant, and professional, reflecting seriousness and decorum. I want the background to be white and the text black. I want it to have a contemporary look. It will consist of a home screen containing the logo (the logo image of which I will send you), prayer times, the time remaining for each prayer, and the Hijri calendar. It will also include information about days and months, such as the sacred months: Sha'ban, Ramadan, and others, in the form of a calendar and a bottom bar containing buttons for the main screens, the Quran, Hadith, rulings, sound, and counters. All of these are separate screens. The Quran, Hadith, rulings, and images will have their content added later, but nothing will be written on them. The counters screens will contain the numbers, pebbles, and a button for the tasbih pages. Its dedicated button page will be on the counters screen and will contain a counter button and words like Subhan Allah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar, and Iman. On the counters screens, you will need a button called "Preparations" that contains the Quran, breastfeeding, 3D, and an interactive continent. You will need maps of all countries to learn about the Quran in Google Earth. When you click on any country, the preparations will appear. I will provide you with these preparations.
